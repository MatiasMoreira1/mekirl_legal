MekiRL

A desktop studio for Rocket League content creators.

What it does

MekiRL helps a creator manage their whole workflow in one place: video ideas, scripts, a publishing calendar, local clip library, mechanics progress and performance stats.

It also lets the creator prepare a video once and send it to their own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. For TikTok, the video is sent to the creator's own account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, and the creator completes the post inside the TikTok app.

How it works with TikTok

  • The creator logs in with their own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit.
  • The creator chooses a video and caption inside the app.
  • The app sends the video to the creator's TikTok account only when the creator explicitly confirms.

MekiRL is a desktop application. All data stays on the user's computer.
