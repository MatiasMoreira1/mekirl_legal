MekiRL – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

MekiRL ("the App") is a desktop application used by its creator to plan, prepare and publish their own video content to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account, the App receives an access token and basic account information (such as your display name) through TikTok's official login and Content Posting API. The App uses this only to send the videos you choose to your own account.

2. Where data is stored

All data, including access tokens, scripts, ideas and video files, is stored locally on your own computer. The App does not operate any server and does not upload your data to any service other than the platform you are publishing to.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose your data to third parties. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, Instagram or YouTube when you explicitly choose to publish.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect TikTok at any time from the App's settings or by removing the App's access in your TikTok account settings (Settings and privacy > Security & permissions > Apps and websites). Deleting the App's local data removes all stored tokens.

5. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

6. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
